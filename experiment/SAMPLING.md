## What this shows, and what it cannot

Repeating a verdict shows repeatability, not correctness. Postings 1 and 4 got "relevant" on all five tries, and my label says "not_relevant" (a required specialty Jane lacks): the prompt applies its seven checks the same way every time, and those checks say nothing about specialties. Postings 7 and 8 got my label's verdict on all five tries.

The variation that did appear was in the format, not the verdict. Three of the five replies on posting 5 held two answers: a first JSON object saying "not_relevant" with a confused justification, then a line beginning "Correction", then a second object saying "relevant". The validator rejected all three as not one JSON object, as the contract requires, so they count as no valid judgment; a looser parser would have kept one of the two answers without saying so. Both valid replies on posting 5 matched my label.

What these 25 calls cannot show: five tries per posting cannot pin down a rate (3 of 5 has a 95% Wilson interval of 23% to 88%); the five postings come from one board on one day, chosen because the rule passes them, not at random; and another model, prompt or day could behave differently. Agreement with my label is agreement with one person's reading.
